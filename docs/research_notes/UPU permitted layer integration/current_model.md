# Current permitted-layer model

## What can be reused for a UPU permitted layer?

### Takeaway
Consensus Builder already has the right read model: a source-derived, read-only Permitted representation with evidence, authority and applicability separate from an authored design. It must be extended as one planning-source/evaluation contract; an official UPU should not be turned into an authored urban-rule design or a second map layer.

### Cited Findings
- The vocabulary distinguishes a source planning rule from an authored urban-rule design, and defines zoning areas as source observations that may overlap. It also says an obligatory source representation is not itself proof of compliance. — [vocabulary: source rules and zoning](../../vocabulary.md#L554-L604)
- The three displayed roles are observed Built, source-derived Permitted, and browser-applied Proposed. Zagreb's Permitted geometry comes from saved future-building results; a display variation does not modify source data or an authored design. — [vocabulary: planning display roles](../../vocabulary.md#L699-L701)
- The provider registry exposes city-neutral `planning-representations.v1` through query, record and parcel reads. Each provider is explicit, rather than inferred from its geometry. — [planning source registry](../../../backend/planning/sources.js#L18-L38); [representation query](../../../backend/planning/sources.js#L71-L112)
- Source representations receive a stable SHA-256 content revision via `withSourceProposal`; the client can retain a source record as evidence without treating it as an applied change. — [source revision adapter](../../../backend/planning/source-proposal.js#L1-L18); [client source proposal contract](../../../frontend/js/planning/source-proposal.js)
- The inspector exposes source label, computed/fetched time, solver version, parcel identifier, source terms, references, and source URL. That is already the right explainability surface for UPU document/page citations and map-feature references. — [planning inspector](../../../frontend/js/planning/views.js#L370-L406)
- The existing Zagreb provider is read-only and uses `public.parcel_build_envelope`, not a browser-side rule interpreter. Its record has source edition, hash, solver version, rule, cache status and a `sourceEvidence` payload. — [Zagreb provider](../../../backend/planning/providers/zagreb-gup.js#L1-L16); [representation assembly](../../../backend/planning/providers/zagreb-gup.js#L55-L87)
- A source-derived permitted envelope keeps legal/calculation qualifications. The outer-bound model can already represent alternative configurations, stepped tiers, conditional extents and an unknown street-facing edge. — [vocabulary: envelopes and conditions](../../vocabulary.md#L602-L610); [vocabulary: frontage assumption](../../vocabulary.md#L630-L630)

### Inferences
- Reuse `planning-representations.v1` for each evaluated UPU unit: `role: 'permitted'`, geometry/volumes, `source` with official edition and frozen revision, authority/applicability, normalized constraints where executable, and raw official evidence. Add a plan/evaluation identity to `source` or `sourceEvidence`; do not create a separate “UPU building” type.
- The compiler must retain every rule’s subject and denominator: e.g. `k_ig` needs the exact ground-area denominator, `k_is` the stated floor-area convention, green-area share the relevant site area, height/storeys their measurement basis, and a frontage condition the exact applicable edge/reference. A naked numeric field is not a reusable constraint.
- Keep opaque official provisions when the compiler cannot safely model them. They remain inspectable evidence and yield `unresolved`/conditional status, rather than being silently omitted or converted to zero capacity.

### Gaps
- No current provider contract represents an official plan-unit polygon, its map-sheet/page anchor, a textual provision anchor, or rule composition across multiple UPU overlays. Those must be introduced with the import/evaluation model.
- The raw currently deployed GUP cache does not establish which source variables are exposed in every row; current code must not be presented as an executable mapping of Croatian coefficients.

## How do parcel cuts, plan geometry and source versioning fit the present runtime?

### Takeaway
The live parcel fabric already preserves cadastral anchors and validates full coverage for authored changes, so it can display and analyse a UPU boundary cutting a parcel. It is not, however, the legal applicability resolver: an official UPU polygon must be intersected with each complete cadastral parcel during evaluation, while the result reports the intersection and uncertainty rather than inventing a new cadastral lot.

### Cited Findings
- Cadastral ground is immutable input; `LiveParcelFabric` is a derived mutable presentation of cadastral ground, cuts and proposal-produced faces. It is expressly not a replacement cadastre. — [parcel fabric implementation](../../../frontend/js/parcels/live-fabric.js)
- Multi-part cadastral geometry gets a deterministic `#cadastre-n` presentation identity while retaining the one original `cadastreParcelIds` anchor. — [live fabric normalization](../../../frontend/js/parcels/live-fabric.js#L133-L155)
- The fabric stores `cadastreParcelIds`, producer identities and derived revisions, indexes by cadastral identity, and provides `entriesForCadastre`, `producedBy`, bounds queries and subscriptions. — [fabric identity/index fields](../../../frontend/js/parcels/live-fabric.js#L59-L78); [published runtime surface](../../../frontend/js/parcels/live-fabric.js#L1112-L1179)
- Replacement operations reject material overlap and uncovered source ground; equal aggregate area cannot hide a hole/overlap. — [replacement coverage checks](../../../frontend/js/parcels/live-fabric.js#L634-L645); [parcel fabric implementation](../../../frontend/js/parcels/live-fabric.js)
- Current general envelope evaluation reads complete cadastral lots in and around the viewport and determines zoning parts by geometric overlap. It deliberately emits one bound for each major zoning part, clipped to that part, rather than pretending a parcel belongs to just one zone. — [parcel envelope evaluation](../../../backend/planning/parcel-envelopes.js#L198-L229); [per-part result construction](../../../backend/planning/parcel-envelopes.js#L245-L276); [outer-bound definition](../../vocabulary.md#L606-L608)
- A current design document specifies a reusable plan-plot identity: a content-hashed plot with the contributing cadastral IDs and producer, keyed as `envelope:<source>:<plotId>`. It specifies replacement of source lots and provenance instead of duplicate bounds. — [plan-plot design](../../permitted-plan-parcels.md#1-one-permitted-pipeline-three-parcel-origins)
- That document confirms a material limitation: Zagreb GUP presently reads cached per-cadastral-parcel results and has no arbitrary-geometry solve endpoint. The proposed shared pipeline would use the GUP adapter to evaluate a supplied polygon as a hypothetical plot. — [Zagreb rule adapter](../../permitted-plan-parcels.md#4-zagreb-and-nyc-rule-adapters)

### Inferences
- For an official UPU rule boundary that crosses a parcel, the primary development unit stays the cadastral parcel. Store the plan unit/overlay polygon unchanged, calculate `parcel ∩ applicability-area` as a source-derived applicability component, and retain both geometries and their source revisions. Do not write this intersection back as a parcel or make it a live-fabric replacement.
- The result should make the outcome explicit: (a) rule applies to whole parcel by the plan’s legal rule, (b) it applies to the intersected part, (c) the plan requires future parcel formation/land readjustment, or (d) the source is indeterminate at this scale. Only (b) produces clipped geometry; (a), (c), and (d) need different statuses and cannot be inferred just from `ST_Intersects`.
- When an official plan actually defines new building plots, import them as immutable plan units, anchored to source geometry and to every affected cadastre ID. Reuse the proposed plan-plot identity/provenance contract, but keep it distinct from mutable live-fabric pieces and label any envelope calculated on it as a plan-geometry what-if unless the UPU itself makes that plot legally operative.
- Versioned GUP/UPU layers must resolve through an immutable effective plan definition, not an integer year or `current=true`. A base plan plus amendments/overrides, with explicit precedence and spatial scope, directly answers overlapping plan layers.

### Gaps
- `POST /planning/envelopes` with plan plots, fabric-driven invalidation, the shared parcel input for Zagreb, and the matching MCP path are proposed in `docs/permitted-plan-parcels.md`, but the observed route remains GET-only and the client feed sends GET requests. — [observed route](../../../backend/routes/planning.js#L27-L48); [observed feed](../../../frontend/js/planning/feed.js#L8-L31)
- The current direct-dimensions pipeline refuses stacked zoning areas (`study-multiple-zoning-areas`), so it cannot be reused unchanged for UPU base-plus-overlay composition. — [current envelope logic](../../../backend/planning/parcel-envelopes.js#L245-L259)

## What system extension is required for executable Croatian rules and MCP parity?

### Takeaway
The system needs a plan compiler and evaluator between imported UPU sources and the existing permitted representation. It should resolve a frozen effective definition—including priority, exceptions, deferrals and map/text citations—then produce parcel results with explicit non-executable and conditional states. The existing authoring rules and MCP building tools are useful consumers, not a Croatian-rule compiler.

### Cited Findings
- The existing authored Detached rule supports a bounded subset of dimensional controls: distance/yards, height/storeys, plot size/frontage, site coverage, natural ground, FAR, and ground-floor area. It creates a maximum envelope and a separate illustrative build-out. — [vocabulary: Detached rule](../../vocabulary.md#L568-L572)
- An authored urban rule is categorically different from an official source planning rule; matching geometry or a name does not make the authored rule enacted. — [vocabulary distinction](../../vocabulary.md#L554-L576)
- The GUP integration contract says official text, maps and exceptions feed a rule source and then a solver; direct Consensus Builder authoring enters through a spatial-proposal adapter, with both publishing common spatial results. — [shared-boundary diagram and ownership](../../gup-scenario-integration.md#L68-L88)
- The same document requires result units to retain geometry, capacity/use, min/max/exact semantics, uncertainty, provenance, explicit floors/areas, source cadastral anchors and diagnostics. It explicitly distinguishes permitted boundaries, a chosen maximum, and an illustration, and prohibits treating missing output as zero. — [result contract](../../gup-scenario-integration.md#L64-L66); [unit result requirements](../../gup-scenario-integration.md#L88-L90)
- It specifies the needed version/precedence model: frozen base revision plus amendments, separate rule definitions and assignment polygons, retained exceptions/deferrals, explicit conflict resolution, and an effective definition before solving. — [base plus amendments](../../gup-scenario-integration.md#L92-L98); [definition/evaluation contents](../../gup-scenario-integration.md#L125-L135)
- It further specifies sparse overrides over a compatible base evaluation and says a non-buildable/unresolved override shadows its base; read precedence cannot use `COALESCE(new_geometry, base_geometry)`. — [incremental evaluation rules](../../gup-scenario-integration.md#L137-L155)
- Current MCP `ugt_draft_building { permitted: true }` calls `GET /planning/envelopes` on the intended building site and requires exactly one envelope; `fit_to_permitted` repeats this through the design’s single parcel binding. — [MCP draft flow](../../../backend/agents/mcp-drafts.js#L102-L108); [MCP fit flow](../../../backend/agents/mcp-drafts.js#L181-L187)
- MCP already exposes authored urban-rule drafting and documents coupled rule-massing edits, but it has no import/inspect/edit primitive for official planning source definitions or scoped official-rule overrides. — [MCP tool surface](../../../backend/agents/mcp-companion.mjs#L104-L136); [MCP editing contract](../../mcp-editing.md#L226-L227)

### Inferences
- Minimal new shared contract: `planning_plan` (stable identity), immutable `planning_definition_revision` (official source manifest and typed operations), immutable `planning_feature` (verbatim imported plan geometry/text locator), `planning_evaluation` (frozen compiler/solver/cadastre/context versions), and `planning_unit_result` (one effective result or status per cadastral parcel, plus optional legally defined plan unit). This follows the existing scenario design, rather than adding a parallel permitted database.
- Import rules in three layers: (1) authoritative artefacts and locators; (2) normalized rule assertions with value, unit, subject, denominator, condition and citation; (3) executable expressions whose compiler target is documented. The compiler returns `executable`, `conditional`, `source-only`, `unresolved`, `not-applicable`, `no-new-build`, `pending-required-procedure`, or `failed`—never an implicit zero.
- Rule selection must have a deterministic total order independent of import timing: jurisdiction/plan hierarchy, legal effective interval, explicit amendment/supersession relation, spatial scope, then a named rule-specific composition operator. Overlapping overlays should compose only where the source defines composition; otherwise result status is conflict/unresolved with both citations.
- Extend MCP in the same release as human editing: discover plan definitions/evaluations, inspect a unit’s effective rule stack and citations, import a source package or create a reviewed draft, edit typed assertions/scopes, request a bounded preview or evaluation, and use an evaluated permitted result in `fit_to_permitted`. Do not expose free-form source prose as if it were safely executable.

### Gaps
- No source-to-IR compiler or production persistence for the plan-definition/evaluation tables exists in this checkout; `docs/gup-scenario-integration.md` is an open design, not evidence of deployed behavior.
- The Zagreb provider currently normalizes only an optional dwelling maximum as a constraint. `k_is`, `k_ig`, landscape/green, frontage, parking, procedure and conditional rules are either embedded in cached solver output/evidence or unsupported by this adapter. — [current normalized constraint](../../../backend/planning/providers/zagreb-gup.js#L80-L86)
- The exact legal interpretation of each UPU rule and the relationship of map scale to cadastral boundaries must come from the official plan package and its implementation provisions; runtime geometry alone cannot resolve it.
