# Developer crowdfunding

A developer can raise devnet USDC for one published plan revision by creating a **Developer crowdfunding** agreement. The developer attaches the contract that describes what a contributor receives. For example, a contract could specify how to calculate an investor’s share of future rental income, when it is payable and who must pay it. The developer supplies the actual terms. The plan, contract, developer wallet, minimum funding target, deadline and the lens (its members and how many must confirm) are committed together under the agreement’s content identifier (CID).

## From proposal to settlement

1. Open the named plan and choose **Developer crowdfunding**. Enter a target and deadline, optionally list up to eight lens members and how many of them must confirm, and attach the investor contract. The attachment can be a PDF or UTF-8 text file up to 256 KiB. Publish with the developer wallet connected. Anyone who opens the agreement can download those exact bytes. A change to the plan or contract creates a different agreement.
2. The developer mints that agreement on Solana devnet. An investor opens the agreement, downloads the contract and reviews the target and deadline. The funding bar shows contributions less refunds against the target, including the full amount raised when the target is exceeded. The time bar stays full until the final ten days, then shrinks toward the displayed deadline. The time remaining is shown throughout the round.
3. The investor selects **Contribute**, enters an amount and connects a Solana wallet in the same form. A guest can participate without creating a profile. Selecting **Contribute** in the form asks the wallet to sign after a successful simulation. Once the proposal escrow records the deposit, a confirmation dialog shows the amount. The same amount stays beside **Contribute** for that wallet in the current tab.
4. Once contributions reach the target, anyone selects **Confirm funding**. The agreement program reads the round’s USDC escrow itself and records the target as met; no lens is involved. When the round names a lens, its threshold of members must also confirm, for investor eligibility or any other condition they are asked to check. A round without a lens settles on its funding target alone. Anyone can then execute the agreement through its deadline, and the escrow can release the funds to the published developer wallet.
5. While the on-chain decision is pending, the developer can cancel. After the deadline, anyone can expire an unexecuted agreement. In either case, each investor can reclaim their deposits. Delivering the return promised in the attachment remains the developer’s obligation under that contract.

The investor needs devnet USDC for the contribution and devnet SOL in the same wallet for transaction fees and the accounts the deposit creates. Both are available free. Enter the wallet’s public address at each faucet and choose **USDC** and **Solana Devnet** at Circle. The contribution form keeps the amount through wallet connection, a declined signature or a failed simulation. [Solana devnet faucet](https://faucet.solana.com/) · [Circle testnet faucet](https://faucet.circle.com/)

## Existing records and programs

| Need | Existing primitive and its role |
| --- | --- |
| A proposal to finance | The exact named plan referenced by `agreement/v1.planCid` supplies the designs. A developer can raise funds for it without assigning land or collecting owner votes. |
| Identifiable terms | The agreement rule uses `electorate: "investors"`, `binding: true`, a lens of zero to eight member keys and an optional `lensThreshold`. The agreement sponsor is the developer, who may also be a lens member; the agreement then says that the developer is one of the verifiers. The usual agreement publication and share link carry these terms. |
| A target and the investor’s contract | The agreement’s `funding.targetUsdcAtomic` is a positive whole number of millionths of a USDC, stored as a decimal string. Its `funding.contract` contains the filename, media type and original bytes in base64. The bytes are covered by the agreement CID and travel with the agreement. |
| Deposits and refunds | The existing `proposal_pledge` program supplies the USDC escrow and per-contribution positions. The agreement program’s `confirm_funding` counts the USDC recorded for those positions, less refunds, and checks the vault covers them. An unsolicited transfer can increase the balance paid at release. It earns no position and contributes zero towards the target. |
| Approval and release | The agreement program fixes the lens, its threshold, the USDC target, the deadline and the developer payout address at mint. Its native SOL requirement is zero. The program checks the funding target itself and the lens members’ decisions, and the escrow checks the agreement’s terminal state on release or refund. |

Every lens member and the developer payout address appear beside the contract, with one line such as “Any 1 of 3 must confirm”. Selecting an address opens its devnet account in Solana Explorer in a new tab. `ugt_inspect_agreement` returns the same terms in `investorReview`. The program checks the funding target. The lens checks investor eligibility and any other conditions; the reference lens issues no automatic decision for a round. In a round without apartment inventory, any wallet can contribute. The first flow ends when escrow pays the developer or investors reclaim their deposits. The attached contract defines what the developer owes after payout.

The Alatau research describes how to verify a funding-only target inside the program, when a round needs an external attestation, and which legal records support a promise of an apartment. [Alatau investment and apartment rights](reports/Alatau%20investment%20and%20apartment%20rights.md)

## Apartment reservations

A developer can offer one to eight whole apartments in a crowdfunding agreement. All units share the plan revision, developer contract, funding target, lens and deadline. An apartment round needs at least one lens member to qualify buyers. Each has a fixed reservation deposit and a full price, both in devnet USDC. The developer specifies how the deposit is credited and what happens at contracting and delivery in the attached terms.

1. In **Developer crowdfunding**, enter the project’s permanent ID and choose **Add apartment**. Paste the apartment link copied from X-ray, enter a permanent unit ID and buyer-facing label, set the price and deposit, and attach the unit’s PDF drawing. The sum of the offered deposits must cover the target.
2. Publish and mint the agreement. From the apartment list the developer signs **List apartment** for each unit. The first listing opens the round’s USDC escrow. Each listing binds the price, contract, drawing, deposit and agreement account to the unit. A revised round can list an unreserved unit under the same permanent IDs.
3. Choose **View apartment in X-ray** or **Download drawing** before reserving. A buyer entering from X-ray can use **Find apartment offers**. **Reserve apartment** opens the price, deposit, drawing and developer contract together. Connect the buyer wallet in that review. **Copy qualification request** gives the buyer, agreement, the round’s lens members and exact unit to share with the lens. A lens member decides whether the buyer qualifies under its policy. After its approval, the buyer signs one transaction that deposits USDC and reserves the unit. A pending request leaves the apartment available to other qualified buyers.
4. One Solana transaction pays the deposit and claims the whole unit. Competing reservations share an account keyed by developer wallet, project ID and unit ID. Only the winning transaction deposits USDC. Retrying a confirmed claim returns its position. The buyer sees a confirmation and can return with the same wallet to **Download contract schedule**. That JSON file includes the original contract and drawing, reviewed X-ray locator, plan and agreement CIDs, full price, paid deposit, buyer, developer and on-chain accounts. A transaction signature is included when available in the current tab.
5. Only matching funded reservations count towards the round’s target. A successful payout leaves every reservation with its buyer. On cancellation or expiry a buyer can take back the deposit. Clearing a refunded position frees the apartment for a new listing. The downloadable contract schedule includes the buyer, agreement CID, unit, deposit position and the lens attestation address.

The identity follows the apartment across rounds or floor-plan revisions under the same developer wallet. The X-ray locator and drawing identify precisely what was reviewed for each reservation. A generated apartment also carries its geometry fingerprint. An updated drawing or address requires a new published agreement and listing. The developer controls the permanent inventory identifiers. The application records that developer’s allocation, while the applicable purchase contract and land records govern legal ownership. [Building and apartment links](building-floor-models.md)

The optional inventory lives in `funding.apartments`. Each unit’s full price and deposit are decimal strings of USDC millionths. The agreement CID commits the inventory and all attachments. The pledge program stores a shared `ApartmentUnit` PDA and the existing donation position is the payment receipt. Only funded reservations count towards the target that `confirm_funding` checks. Agents can create or revise the inventory with the existing crowdfunding tools, ask `ugt_inspect_agreement` for `includeAvailability`, and prepare the same wallet actions with `ugt_approval_link` and a `unitId`.

The reference lens operator grants the approval with `POST /lens/agreements/:cid/apartments/:unitId/qualify` and a body containing the buyer’s public address in `buyer`. The buyer reads it at `GET /lens/agreements/:cid/apartments/:unitId/qualification?buyer=<wallet>`. The same `PropertyOfferDecision-v2` schema used for standalone offers binds the agreement CID, unit account, terms hash, buyer and developer. Its payout hash commits the escrow vault and the USDC deposit in base units. Solana verifies that the signer is one of the round’s lens members, the expiry and the terms before the transaction can commit. Any member may qualify a buyer. This approval is separate from the round’s funding target, which the program confirms.

The apartment inventory layout includes the qualification address. Existing experimental apartment listings need to be recreated for this layout. Regular funding contributions retain their existing payment flow. [Standalone apartment offers](property-offers.md)

Opening a live round with this variation requires the version of the pledge program that supports apartment listing and reservation. The ordinary contribution instructions use their existing program interface.

The agreement account layout with a lens set and a funding target is not deployed yet. Agreements minted under the earlier one-lens layout cannot be read by the current codec; recreate them once the new program is deployed. [Agreement program](../blockchain/solana/programs/agreement/README.md)
