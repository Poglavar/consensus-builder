# Alatau investment and apartment rights

A developer can raise money for a proposal and give investors a contractual claim on the project. The existing agreement already has a place to record that promise in the investor contract attached to a named plan revision. For a first round, identify the company, its payout wallet, the funding target, the deadline and exactly what each contribution buys. A share of rent, a vote in a company and delivery of an apartment require different promises and evidence. [Developer crowdfunding](../developer-crowdfunding.md)

## Which land can a proposal use?

Start with the adopted plan boundary or an existing cadastral parcel. **Use this area as a design site** takes the selected zoning feature’s complete geometry, including holes and separate parts. `ugt_planning_areas` and the `planningArea` draft parameter expose the same source to agents. General-plan record `alatau-genplan-727:479341`, for example, identifies a residential area. A design can cite the provider, record and revision, and separately bind the cadastral parcels it actually touches. It can show a development opportunity without cutting a plot on apparently empty land. On the house-demo parcel the source identifies homestead housing with a private plot. Its legend gives no storey count. The app’s three-storey assumption and the original proposer’s five-storey assumption are both visible if you inspect the source. To mint long virtual-parcel IDs, the parcel program and its callers would need to agree on a fixed 32-byte seed such as a digest of the full namespaced ID. Keep the full ID and its geometry evidence in the record. A mint identifies the subject and supplies no land right. ## Who bids, who gets paid, and what investors receive

Alatau’s special law covers a **fund of unallocated state land**. Article 67(4) provides for paid use by residents, and Article 67(9) allows an exceptional route to purchase after satisfying the investment and business-licence conditions. Article 67(7) leaves the allocation procedure to administration rules. Loss of the licence or early termination of the project agreement can end the relevant lease. Article 90 brings those provisions and the main resident-business rules into force on **1 January 2027**. The sandbox provision, Article 54, has applied since **1 July 2026**. The special residential-property and cadastral provisions in Articles 65–66 commence **1 July 2027**. Existing rights and projects have their own transition rules. For a specific bid, the grant, licence, lease and applicable administration acts determine the actual term and work promised. [Consolidated Alatau law, Articles 39–40, 54, 65–67, 78–83 and 90](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2600000286)

**Use the eligible developer company as the lead.** A company can bid, hold a lease and owe investors money. Name it in the contract and show evidence that it controls the payout wallet. A founder of a Kazakhstan limited liability partnership generally risks the value of their contribution. Unpaid capital, a personal guarantee or statutory liability can change that result. A simple partnership has no separate legal personality. Partners manage by common consent unless they delegate, and their project obligations to third parties are generally joint and several unless the agreement provides otherwise. An existing developer avoids creating an entity merely to publish a proposal. The statutory housing routes below have a specific single-project authorised-company requirement. [Civil Code, Articles 44, 228–231](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K940001000_) · [LLP Law, Article 2](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z980000220_)

| Investor’s intended return | What the developer must specify |
| --- | --- |
| Share of rental or sale proceeds | Percentage, revenue or profit base, deductible costs and developer fee, payment dates, accounts the investor can inspect and remedies for nonpayment. |
| Debt or company equity | For debt, maturity, repayment priority and security. For equity, actual issuance or transfer and the class’s voting, distribution and loss rights. |
| A particular apartment | The exact unit, price, delivery conditions and registered legal instrument. Pooling proportional contributions alone cannot allocate a whole apartment to each contributor. |

A proposer can earn a disclosed design or development fee, retain a defined share, or win a commission. A development fee carries no automatic right to manage investor money. An urban rule describes what can be designed. An obligation to build comes from the signed project or construction contract, licence or land grant. The original disabled **Joint Investment** button was specified for an initial Purchase-only interface. The record supplies no legal reason for disabling it. [Original proposal brief](../../TODO-proposals.txt)

For a **conditional bid**, investors can commit first. The lead then submits the official bid with the land price, construction budget, committed equity, finance commitments and contingency. The tender determines which evidence counts as proof of funds. If winning by a deadline is an explicit condition, release once to the company-controlled wallet on verified success and let each investor reclaim their position on failure or expiry. The current funding-only agreement releases on funding approval, so it cannot promise a refund for losing a later bid. Even a genuinely conditional deposit ties up capital and incurs fees. After payout the investors bear the company, construction, financing and delivery risks described in their instrument. Showing credible construction finance up front does not require drawing all of it into the first escrow. [Current agreement flow](../developer-crowdfunding.md)

## What should the lens verify?

**The funding total can be checked entirely on chain.** Today the contract bytes, USDC minimum, plan revision and deadline are covered by the agreement CID. The Solana agreement account accepts one lens decision. The reference lens compares finalized, credited deposits with the target and checks the escrow vault covers them. The agreement program itself trusts that decision for the USDC target. An unsolicited token transfer does not count towards the target or create an investor position. On cancellation or expiry, each investor can claim their recorded deposits. [Agreement program](../../blockchain/solana/programs/agreement/src/lib.rs) · [Contribution escrow](../../blockchain/solana/programs/proposal_pledge/src/lib.rs) · [Current contract](../developer-crowdfunding.md)

The next funding rule should commit the target, canonical USDC mint and designated escrow on chain. At settlement the program should check the net credited contributions and the vault backing in the same transaction. Anyone could then finalize a fully funded round with no external conditions. Keep the lens visible on the agreement. For a real external condition, freeze a bounded list of distinct addresses and a threshold at mint, count each member once, and bind every attestation to the exact agreement and fact. A named developer address counts. Before deposit show, for example, **“The developer is one of three verifiers. Any two must agree on the land award.”** Existing software has a single on-chain verifier and some mint paths refuse a sponsor’s own key. A multi-address threshold and consistent self-selection require a coordinated program and client change. [Agreement state](../../blockchain/solana/programs/agreement/src/lib.rs) · [Agent minting path](../../backend/agents/agreement-minter.js)

Investor eligibility is a separate decision. If an offering requires identity or investor qualification, verify a wallet-bound credential **before accepting its deposit**, including through sponsored transactions and direct program calls. Show who verified eligibility, what it covers and when it expires. Keep personal documents off chain. For an unrestricted test round, show that any wallet can contribute, which key decides funding, the payout wallet, the minimum target, the treatment of excess deposits and the refund deadline. A capped entitlement needs an allocation or refund rule before the last available position is sold.

## Can buyers finance apartments before completion?

**Yes. Buyers can use savings, and a participating bank finances the developer’s project.** Kazakhstan’s Law No. 486‑V governs shared participation in housing construction. A *DDU* is the buyer’s contract for a defined share, typically the future apartment. Articles 7–10 provide three routes. [Shared-construction law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z1600000486)

| Route | When a project can take purchasers’ funds |
| --- | --- |
| Unified Operator guarantee | The qualifying project obtains the statutory completion guarantee. Kazakhstan Housing Company is the Unified Operator. |
| Participating commercial bank | The bank commits sufficient financing to finish. The bank, authorised company and engineering company enter their agreement, and the project obtains local permission. Buyer money and bank lending can then be used during construction. Each buyer decides separately how to finance their purchase. |
| Completed frame | An experienced developer finances construction through the structural skeleton. The load-bearing elements, floor slabs and covering structure must be in place. An engineering report confirms the frame, and local permission is required. Fit-out and commissioning can follow. |

The buyer signs the electronic DDU, which takes effect upon recording by the local authority. Article 14 directs noncash payment to the authorised company’s bank account. Article 20 governs accounts and permitted outflows, including engineering confirmation of completed work. Its 10% cap applies to the specified *other project expenses* in Article 20(8)(3). Article 7(2) expressly covers construction funding collected from individuals, legal entities and simple partnerships. Calling an apartment presale a token, reservation or investment does not supply an additional route. A corporate pre-bid instrument needs its own classification according to what it actually funds and promises. A devnet USDC transfer cannot take the place of the registered DDU payment. [Law No. 486‑V, Articles 7, 11–14 and 20](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z1600000486) · [Ministry’s purchaser guidance](https://www.gov.kz/org/kds/press/news/details/1296454?lang=ru) · [Housing Portal](https://homeportal.kz/)

**What happens when the developer’s lease expires?** Ordinary temporary paid land use can run up to five years or, for a long-term right, from five to 49 years, subject to exceptions. A qualifying leaseholder can have priority for renewal. During construction the land right and its expiry matter to delivery. Once apartments are separately owned and the necessary plot enters the condominium’s common property, Housing Relations Act Article 31 provides for termination of the developer’s previous land-use right. Land Code Article 62 gives each unit an inseparable share of the common property. In that ordinary registered case, the former lease’s expiry date is no countdown to the owners losing their homes. For an unfinished building, an unregistered interest or land outside the common plot, examine the actual register and lease. [Housing Relations Act, Article 31](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z970000094_) · [Land Code, Articles 35, 37 and 62](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K030000442_)

## Giving one apartment a permanent identity

The apartment-history search covered **26 October through 24 November 2026**. It recovered the decision to share published floor drawings across apps. X-ray now reads `buildings.floor_plan` through the existing floor-plan API. The publication identifies the building, registers drawings with four geographic corners and locates units within storeys. In an actual published-source example, `A1-1` appears on `A1-floor-1` and points to its own drawing. Repeated layouts have distinct apartment occurrences. Generated layouts are marked `suggested`. The review found no equivalent verified Alatau unit schedule. Link each contract to the exact plan revision, building, storey and unit the investor reviewed. Enforce one active reservation per permanent unit across every revision and round. The official address, registered title and eventual handover are separate references. In an IFC source retain the apartment group’s `GlobalId` and its `IfcSpace` members. ## Tokens, title and disputes

**KASE** is the Kazakhstan Stock Exchange. Its memorandum with Alatau City Authority explores a tokenized-asset platform. The Authority describes land, bonds, private credit and construction finance as pilots being developed under its *Tokenization by Default* concept. These publications establish the city’s policy direction. They do not identify a completed tokenized sale of the demo parcel or apartment. [KASE memorandum](https://kase.kz/en/media-center/events/zq8vuw1bme002vmpip6zdhqq) · [City Authority’s programme](https://alataucity.kz/en/news-alatau-city-presents-the-tokenization-by-default-concept-at-the-14th-congress-of-kazakhstans-financiers.html)

Kazakhstan’s Digital Assets Law distinguishes the asset, underlying claim and licensed or registered operators. The real-property registration law governs title. Alatau Article 53 addresses the evidentiary role of compliant digital records. Article 66(3) permits the administration to establish a blockchain legal cadastre. The reviewed instruments designate no general-purpose public chain as that register. A stolen private key can move a freely transferable token while the title register continues to name the registered owner. Freezing or reissuing a token depends on the issuer’s lawful powers, platform rules, custody and applicable orders. A title correction follows the registry and legal process. Article 74 sets a negotiated and judicial or arbitral route for qualifying investment disputes. The cited statutes provide no universal private-key recovery or automatic reissue. This leaves room for the product to attest an official record on Solana while the legal record and its remedies stay identifiable. [Digital Assets Law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2300000193) · [Real-property registration law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z070000310_) · [Alatau law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2600000286)
