Agreement walkthrough Lens member statements

An agreement names its lens: the lens members whose statements it accepts. Owners approve with their own wallet, lens members vouch for them and confirm the rule was met, and the agreement on Solana then pays out.

    What the agreement needs

      How this was checked
      • Owner approvals: your browser checks each owner’s wallet signature and each lens member’s signature on it.
      • Lens member statements: read from Solana and checked against the agreement’s account. A statement that a lens member’s service lists but Solana has not recorded is marked as reported.
      • Execution: read from the agreement’s account on Solana devnet.

      That proves who signed what and when. It does not make any statement true: you decide whether to trust the lens members.
