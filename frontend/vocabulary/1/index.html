What the records mean

Version 1 defines the terms used by the published design and agreement JSON-LD views. Records, geographic extents and declarations have distinct meanings.

Read a published record

Append ?format=jsonld to a design or agreement detail URL on the API: /designs/{cid}?format=jsonld or /agreements/{cid}?format=jsonld. Each response includes its context, so a consumer can expand it without fetching a remote context.

Record IRIs use the deployment's configured canonical API URL and the record's CID, the content identifier of its canonical bytes; an agreement references one exact plan CID, and that plan names its member designs.

An authored site is not a legal parcel boundary. A decision rule says who decides, not that anyone has decided. These exports make no assertion of ownership, consent or physical construction.

Record and reference classes

Published design

cb:Design

One authored change (a road, a park, a parcel layout, an urban rule, a building), identified by the CID of its canonical content. Its existence does not assert construction, ownership or legal approval.

Published agreement

cb:Agreement

An arrangement with a decision rule, optional allocations and money, identified by the CID of its canonical content and linked to one exact plan. Its existence does not assert consent, execution or legal approval.

Published plan

cb:Plan

An immutable selection of designs and a saved map view, identified by the CID of its canonical content.

Design site

cb:DesignSite

The geographic extent authored as the design site. It is not a legal cadastral parcel or an assertion of a constructed project.

Declared cadastral reference

cb:CadastreReference

A cadastral identifier an agreement names as its input land. This node is scoped to the agreement; it does not establish cross-provider parcel identity.

Relationships and values

Content identifier

cb:cid

The CIDv1 (UnixFS file, raw leaves, sha2-256, base32) of the record's canonical bytes: its identity. Changed content is a different record.

Range: xsd:string

Author label

cb:authorLabel

A stored author attribution string; not a verified person, organization or signing authority.

Range: xsd:string

Declared design goal

cb:declaredGoal

The exact design-goal string carried by the design, including unclassified values.

Range: xsd:string

Design-goal concept

cb:designGoal

An exact match to the current design-goal concept scheme; not a physical-object classification.

Range: skos:Concept

Authored site

cb:site

The geographic site declared by this design.

Range: cb:DesignSite

Plan

cb:plan

The exact plan whose design selection the agreement settles.

Range: cb:Plan

Decision rule

cb:decisionRule

Who decides the agreement and whether the decision binds, as its content states it.

Range: skos:Concept

Agreement deadline

cb:agreementDeadline

The deadline carried by the agreement. It does not promise a settlement or construction time.

Range: xsd:dateTime

Declared cadastral reference

cb:cadastreReference

An agreement-scoped declaration of a cadastral identifier of its input land.

Range: cb:CadastreReference

Declared identifier

cb:declaredIdentifier

The exact identifier string in a scoped reference.

Range: xsd:string
