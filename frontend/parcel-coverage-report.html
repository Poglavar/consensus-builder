Methods & sources

What counts as a check?

A recorded investigation counts, including a failed request. Check dates come from the linked investigation, not from the date this report was built. An identified source and a verified parcel response are tracked separately.

How is growth calculated?

The UN average annual rate is 100 × ln(population in 2025 / population in 2015) / 10. It describes historical estimates. Forecasts are not mixed into this column, and missing values are never treated as zero.

How are the shares calculated?

Each statistical jurisdiction belongs to one evidence category. Its area and population are summed in that category and divided by the dataset’s world total. National evidence takes precedence over local evidence; unidentified or missing amounts remain unresolved.

What does this leave unanswered?

The actual extent, completeness and freshness of cadastral polygons. Registry discovery cannot establish that every parcel is registered, downloadable, publicly accessible or supported by the app.
