Learn by doing

Draw the route. Build the profile.

Roads and tracks are corridors drawn on the map. Their lanes determine the width, land take and appearance of the finished proposal.

From alignment to road proposal

  1. Draw the alignment

    Choose Road or Track from the Transport tools, or the open-ground menu where available. Click points along the map. The drawing panel reports length, area, intersected parcels, owners, estimated cost and TEAD (estimated acquisition difficulty).

    Undo (U) removes the last point. Finish road (F) completes the line; Cancel stops the drawing. Roads need only the corridor strip through a parcel, so review the affected ground rather than assuming the whole parcel is acquired.

  2. Compose the cross-section

    Open Edit cross-section . Start from a standard profile, then add traffic lanes, sidewalks, cycle paths, parking, green strips, bus lanes or tracks. Select a lane to edit its properties. New lanes are inserted beside the selection; drag their handles to reorder them.

    Total width is the sum of the lanes. Edit individual widths or drag a seam between lanes to transfer width while keeping the total fixed. A reset control restores the standard lane width. Parking options include different layouts and planted spaces; green strips can use grass or trees.

  3. Check fit and crossings

    Review the warnings about buildings and applied public spaces. A building crossing can require choosing surface demolition or a tunnel. The editor indicates when the profile cannot fit the available corridor; a road cannot exceed 80 m in width. Use Apply to drawing to return to the alignment.

  4. Finish and inspect the proposal

    Finish road creates a local proposal and applies it immediately. Click the road to inspect it, use Before / after to check its land take and switch to 3D to review its lanes. If creation fails, the app keeps the drawing for correction.

Tracks and mixed streets

Roads and tracks use the same corridor editor. Track begins with a rail lane; combine it with sidewalks, greenery or traffic lanes for a mixed street. Choose a track gauge of 1000 mm or 1435 mm. For double track, add a second track lane.

Stations and stops

Choose Tram station beside a track, or a compatible station type beside a road. A green preview confirms alignment with a compatible route. Click to place it. Edit geometry opens position and rotation controls; Done saves the revision. Inspect the platform, shelter and surroundings in 3D.

Edit the network

Click an applied road to edit its cross-section or map nodes. Drag a node to move it; Alt/Option-click removes a node. Moving a junction moves its connected legs; Alt/Option-click disconnects them. Drawing up to an existing road connects the network. Bulldozing a middle stretch can split it into separate roads.

Check Applies to before changing a section: the selected segment and the whole road network are different scopes. Use Fork proposal for an alternative design or different terms. Editing a published road changes your local copy; the published or on-chain version stays unchanged. Share and Unapply are in the same proposal panel.

Designate road land

Designate as road land assigns the selected contiguous parcels to road use without drawing an alignment or a lane profile. Use it for the land proposal; use Road or Track when you want to design the corridor itself.
