Use the mode strip at the left of the map. The 3D demonstration at the end of the house video shows the same footprint and surroundings as the 2D map. Right-drag, drag with Ctrl / Cmd, or use two fingers to rotate the abstract 3D view; the wheel zooms.

Control what is visible

Built controls existing buildings; Planned controls proposed buildings, grounds and decorations. Solid and Transparent change opacity; Off hides a group. For Built, Surviving shows what remains after the plan, while Removed shows only demolished buildings or building portions.

Hide real buildings under proposals clears overlapping context. Radius controls the surrounding area loaded, Show all parcels adds boundaries, and Trees and Water control those elements. Facades and facade style add illustrative windows and materials to proposed buildings.

X-ray floor plans

X-ray displays available modelled floors, walls, doors and windows. Cut above selects the highest floor left visible. Floor-plan availability varies by building; the controls report when no plans are available. Heights, opening types and alignment may be inferred or approximate.

Before / after

Choose Before / after in proposal details, the geometry editor or owner review. Drag the divider to expose the original and proposed state on the same map. It compares a change with its starting point; it does not choose between two separate proposals.

Compare two proposals

On a parcel touched by at least two proposals, choose Compare proposals here from its menu, or tick two entries in the parcel’s Proposals tab and click Compare . Choose the proposal on each side to inspect its land take, buildings, resulting plots, ownership, offer and consent. Support rows also show donations, pledges and markets when present.

Read the value estimates

The parcel panel compares built and proposed volume and floor area. Adjust Price per m² to inspect Current value and Proposal gain under your chosen assumption. Proposal info also shows totals and an average per parcel. These are scenario estimates, so inspect each parcel rather than treating the average as a distribution among owners.

Realistic context

Photorealistic coverage depends on the location and whether the tiles load successfully. The app reports unavailable coverage or a load failure. Walk mode is available only where configured; choose a ground position when prompted. Return to 2D for precise parcel selection and editing.

Walk mode is available only in cities where it is configured. Choose a starting position on the ground when prompted. Inspect the proposal at eye level, then return to 2D for precise parcel selection and editing.
