Learn by doing

Make room for public life

Create the surface first, then arrange trees, paths, water and furniture directly on the map. Review the same design in 3D.

Create the space and open its editor

Select parcels or a site, open Propose and choose Park, Square or Lake. Confirm the selected parcel or the surrounding block. The tool creates the surface on the map. Click the result and choose Edit geometry to arrange or revise its furniture.

The furniture editor works directly on the map. Keep every item inside the dashed boundary; drag an item to move it. Geometry revisions carry the design into a new version rather than changing the shared source.

Park: trees, paths and ponds

Choose Tree, Bench, Flowerbed or Pond, then click inside the park. Flowerbeds and ponds occupy a circular area that must fit completely inside the boundary. Zoom in for smaller, more precise placement.

For a Footpath , click its points and choose Finish path or press Enter . Drag the path to reposition it. Use Remove and click an item to delete it.

Square: fountains and furniture

Use Fountain, Tree, Bench, Table and Statue to furnish the square. Choose a type and click inside the outline. Move existing items by dragging them, or use Remove to clear them. The video shows adding a fountain and checking the square in 3D.

Lake: a water surface

Lake creates a water surface across contiguous selected parcels. It is different from the small Pond decoration inside a park. Inspect water together with the surrounding buildings in 3D.

Save and inspect

Save design commits the furniture arrangement; Cancel discards changes from the editing session. Switch to 3D to check the positions. In view controls, Planned includes the public-space surface and decorations; Trees and Water control their visibility.
