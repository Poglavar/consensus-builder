    About

    Consensus Builder is Urban Game Theory's free, map-based planning tool. Hyperstition: Markets for Possible Cities is its hackathon project. Explore real parcels, compare community zoning and development proposals, and build support with the people affected. Read about the open city planning workflow, open the live hackathon demo, read the pitch deck, or see the roadmap.

    Start here

    Build first, propose second. New designs appear on the map immediately, so you can edit, park, compare and share them before adding formal terms. Read the step-by-step guide, or see what works today and what comes next on the roadmap.

    A place to begin

    Shape a city people can agree on

    Choose a city on the globe, or explore anywhere. Inspect parcels and buildings in 2D, 3D, or Photo view where available.

    Open the city picker to travel. Use Layers to choose what you see.

    From idea to proposal

    Make change visible

    Turn an idea into a proposal: reshape parcels, sketch streets and parks, or add buildings. Apply a draft to see how it changes the map.

    Start in Tools, then compare your proposals on the map.

    People make the difference

    A proposal starts a conversation

    Share a plan, gather votes and financial support, and seek acceptance from attested owners. Wallet and identity requirements apply to on-chain actions.

    Open a proposal to inspect its support and acceptance.

    A city in motion

    See how decisions unfold

    Follow proposals and agent activity around the world. Where Simulation is available, play or pause turns to explore how proposals and support evolve.

    Open Activity for the live feed and Simulation controls.

    More than one starting point

    Keep exploring without a register

    Retry an unavailable parcel source or bring your own link. You can also request best-effort boundary estimates from imagery, or generate a reproducible Schelling plan.

    Estimates and generated plans can be wrong. They do not establish legal boundaries.
