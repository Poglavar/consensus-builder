Backing a proposal

Good ideas need
a way to gather support

Funded support

Donate

Commit funds to a specific proposal. Escrow holds the donation until the proposal reaches execution.

Cancellation or expiry allows a refund.

A voluntary commitment

Pledge

Signal what you would contribute. A supporter can revoke a soft pledge or fulfil it after execution.

A pledge becomes funding when its owner follows through.

Support stays connected to the project people can see.

Funding mechanics demonstrated on Solana devnet with test USDC. Community pilots are a next step.05 / 07
