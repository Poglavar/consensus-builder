Open city planning, grounded in real parcels

Consensus Builder is free urban planning software for exploring real parcels and drafting community zoning and development proposals. Compare ideas on a shared map, share a plan, and build support with the people affected. Hyperstition: Markets for Possible Cities is its hackathon project.

The map and local drafting are free to explore. Some agent publishing, funding, or optional blockchain actions may involve fees.

Explore the urban planning map

Community zoning proposals on real ground

Start with cadastral parcels, buildings, and streets. Sketch a block, buildings, a park, a square, a road, or new parcel boundaries, then inspect the proposal in its map context. People can compare alternatives, share proposals, and add support or funding; owners make decisions through the proposal process.

A proposal records an idea for discussion. It does not create an adopted zoning plan, grant a permit, or replace an official planning decision.

A focused map tool for participatory planning

Consensus Builder is a map-based tool for public-facing proposal creation and community participation. It helps people communicate a possible change parcel by parcel, compare designs, and invite responses. Its focus is collaborative planning proposals, with real parcel context and an open process.

Use the planning workflow guide to see how to select land, create a design, revise a proposal, and share the result.

From a map idea to a community proposal

  1. Explore real parcels

    Find the land and surrounding streets or buildings where a change could happen.

  2. Draw and compare alternatives

    Create a local design, adjust its geometry or terms, and inspect how it sits on the map.

  3. Share the proposal

    Publish or share a proposal so the people affected can review it and respond.
