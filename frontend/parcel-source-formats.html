Supported parcel source formats

In the map’s Information panel, choose “Choose a parcel source” and paste a public HTTPS URL. We check the formats below in order, then verify viewport loading and exact parcel lookup before offering the source. Access failures are reported separately from an unsupported format.

Making your source readable

Expose a persistent native parcel key as a property such as PARCEL_ID, parcelNumber, APN, PIN, BBL, FOLIO or cadastral_id. Keep it consistent across spatial and exact-ID requests. Temporary row indexes or ArcGIS object IDs alone do not establish parcel identity.

Every returned geometry must be a valid, closed polygon in longitude/latitude coordinates. Report completeness honestly: truncated responses must expose paging/count information. Raster tiles, WMS images and web viewers alone do not provide parcel geometry.

The URL must be reachable from our proxy over verified HTTPS, on the standard HTTPS port. The URL setup supports anonymous public services; remove embedded credentials and access keys. Private network URLs are not supported. Responses that exceed the size limit or ignore uncompressed transfer requests cannot be checked by this setup flow.

A successful check verifies the small area around your current map center. It does not establish nationwide coverage or source quality. You choose the source and its intended use; terms and attribution remain informational.

Source configuration is saved in this browser for the chosen city and backend. To restore the city’s usual source, choose “Use the city default.” Switching reloads the map; existing proposals retain their original source identity.
