Resolution

Execution by
the deadline.

The agreement account records the outcome and execution time. Execution does not mean construction is complete.

01

YES

The agreement executes on-chain by its stated deadline. Each YES share pays 1; NO pays 0.

02

NO

The agreement expires or misses its execution deadline. Each NO share pays 1; YES pays 0.

03

VOID

The agreement is cancelled. Every share on either side pays 0.5.

Public settlement terms identify the agreement, deadline and payout rule before trading.05 / 07
